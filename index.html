About

NYC-based photographer and self-taught music producer, working since childhood on the sound side and for the past three years behind a camera. Currently moving from a five-year career in software engineering toward hands-on work in production and visual storytelling.

Interested in framing, composition, and the moments a wider shot would miss. Across both mediums, drawn to nonconventional sounds and harmonies, metaphorical abstraction, and unusual framing — each a way of chasing a feeling, not just capturing a moment.

Available for entry-level production, photography assistant, and creative-adjacent work in NYC.
